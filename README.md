# SagTag's Audio Muffler

Havent you thought about certain audios which just seem annoying and you say "i'll get a mod to remove it later" and then you forget? or you remember to add it but you're lazy to restart?

Well trouble no more! **SagTag's Audio Muffler** (*STAM*) lets you silence vanilla and modded audios **without** restarting! in the middle of the cave and you heard the adult maneater footsteps but you dont like them? just press escape, go to audios and search for the audio to mute it! oh you dont want to mute it but you dont wanna hear it at full volume? lower its volume!

the muffled audios stay **stored** until you remove them, and its all clientsided
